# Graduation requirements

What finishing the Academy actually means, stated plainly, so you always know
how far you have left to go.

```{note}
**Prerequisite** None to start checking this page; it applies once you have
begun the domains. **Time to complete the whole programme** ~12 weeks in
cohort form, or self-paced at 4 to 6 hours a week.
```

## The four requirements

You have finished the Academy when all four are true:

1. **All six domains, checked off.** Every domain (Basics through Strategy
   and Governance) ends in its own "Check yourself" list. You do not need a
   passing score on a quiz, you need to be able to honestly tick every box.
2. **One pathway, completed.** Startup or Hospital information, whichever
   matches where you want the work to land.
3. **A capstone project, shipped.** A real tool, built on a real problem,
   evaluated honestly, and presented for review, not just committed to a
   repository nobody opens.
4. **A portfolio, not a transcript.** By the end you are carrying six
   domain artefacts, a pathway project, and a capstone, the things you built,
   not a list of modules you sat through.

```{tip}
If you are self-paced and unsure whether you are actually done with a
domain, the test is not "did I read it." It is "could I rebuild the thing I
made in that domain from memory, badly, in an afternoon." If yes, you
understood it. If no, go back before moving on.
```

## What you do not get

```{warning}
The Academy does not issue an accredited credential or CPD recognition.
Completion here is a real, honestly-evaluated portfolio of work, not a
licence-bearing certificate. If you need something that counts toward
formal continuing education, treat the Academy as preparation for that,
not a replacement for it.
```

We would rather be exact about what this is than borrow authority we have
not earned. What you leave with is proof you can build, reviewed by people
who checked your work, which is worth more in an interview or a Fellowship
application than a certificate with nobody's name behind it.

## Self-paced vs cohort

Both paths lead to the same four requirements.

- **Self-paced.** You are the reviewer. Use each domain's "Check yourself"
  list honestly, and post your capstone in the club's community channels for
  real feedback before you call it finished.
- **Cohort.** Your pod and a mentor review your work at each Review beat, and
  your capstone gets a real presentation. The requirements do not change,
  only who is checking them.

## After you finish

A finished capstone is not filed away. The strongest ones become the seed of
a [Fellowship](../fellowship.html) project, or move toward the
[Venture Studio](../venture.html) if they have real product potential. Most
people who finish do not stop building, they just change which door they
are building behind.
